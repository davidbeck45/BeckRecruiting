## What's built and working today

It runs end-to-end right now. We've tested the whole cycle: athlete profile in → campaign out → coach opened → coach watched film → report to the family.

**1. The coach database.** Every FBS and FCS program syncs automatically from a free public data service (schools, conferences, head coaches). Assistant coaches and their emails come from athletic-site staff directories plus a one-time purchased dataset (~$150 for ~10,000 contacts across all six levels). Every contact is tagged by position group, so a running back's film goes to the RB coach and the recruiting coordinator — not a general inbox. When a contact bounces or a head coach changes, the system flags that school for a refresh. Staying current through the coaching carousel is the moat.

**2. AI-written outreach — one email per coach, in the athlete's voice.** Short (coaches read on phones), references the coach by name and program, leads with the two or three numbers that matter for that position at that level, film link up top, academics included where they matter (D3, service academies, high-academic schools). Hard rule built into the system: **it can never invent a number.** It only uses what we give it — and it prefers your certified numbers (more below).

**3. "Did they see it."** Every email carries an invisible open tracker and a unique film link. Opens are a soft signal; **a film click is a person watching your player.** The family dashboard reads: *"Coach Smith at Demo State opened twice and watched film Thursday at 8:16pm."* Nobody else in this market shows families that. It's also your call sheet — a coach who watched film is a warm phone call.

**4. Your deck's promises, made into machinery.** The Family Night deck commits to specific deliverables, so the system now records them formally:

| Deck promise | In the system |
|---|---|
| Written level projection, every sophomore, every spring | Stored per athlete: level (P4/G5/FCS/D2/D3/NAIA), your written assessment, the film plan, the academic runway |
| "Certified numbers — no inflated stopwatch times" | Each measurable logged with method (laser, coach-certified) and who signed it. The outreach emails cite these and say they're staff-certified |
| **The Receipts** — "[XX]% signed at or above projected level" | Computed automatically from projections vs. signings. The honest version: measured against the *first* projection, the one the family was told up front. **This stat only exists if we record projections from athlete #1 — the system now forces that discipline** |
| "We call every college that signs our players, twice a year" | Signings are stored with the school, so the twice-a-year call list generates itself |
| "15–20 realistic schools, worked coach-to-coach — not mass email" | That's the default. The tiered list gets your calls; the software provides the written first-touch and follow-up to that same list, and tells you which coaches to call first. Wider distribution is a family's explicit opt-in, never the default |

## What we need from you

The software is the easy half. These are the inputs only you have:

1. **Your placement history.** Every past signee — name, school, level, and what you would have projected them at. This backfills The Receipts so the number is real on day one, not "[XX]%".
2. **The certification standard.** What counts as a certified measurable (laser vs. hand-time policy, who on staff can sign one). We built the ledger; you define the bar.
3. **Target-list judgment.** The AI drafts a realistic school board in minutes; you bless or correct it. Your corrections make it smarter about what a D2 corner actually looks like.
4. **The relationship map.** Which programs pick up when you call. Those stay yours — the software works everything else and hands you the warm-call list.
5. **The launch timing call.** Nothing goes to paying families until your All Saints conflict clears (your Aug 4 line). Building and testing quietly now is exactly what your own plan prescribes.

## Guardrails (already baked in)

- Flat fees only — the system has no mechanism for taking a cut of anything.
- No guarantees anywhere — the dashboard shows *process and engagement*, never promised outcomes.
- Every email is truthful — certified numbers only, athlete's real profile, honest voice.
- Coaches can opt out with one click, and an opt-out suppresses them across every athlete, forever. Small tiered sends, never blasts — protecting the sending reputation protects the brand.
- Parent/guardian consent captured at signup; athletes' academic records treated as sensitive.
